YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy at Infinix Shopee.

We respect your privacy and use your information to provide a reliable shopping experience.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as your name, username, and email address.
  • Contact and delivery information needed to communicate about and fulfill orders.
  • Order, returns, seller listing, and customer-support details related to your use of the store.

How information is used

  • To manage your account, process orders, coordinate delivery, and provide order updates.
  • To help customers and sellers resolve questions about products, orders, returns, and exchanges.
  • To maintain store operations, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal requirements.

Sharing and payment information

  • Order details needed for fulfillment may be shared with the relevant seller and delivery providers.
  • Payment information should be processed by a secure payment provider. Full card details should not be stored in your account profile.
  • We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed where required by law.

Your choices and questions

  • Keep your account and delivery details accurate, and contact us to request help with your information.
  • We retain information only as needed to provide service, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.
  • For privacy questions or requests, email infinixshopee@gmail.com.
CLEAR DELIVERY & RETURNS

Shipping & returns policy.

Shipping

  • Enter an Indian delivery address with state, PIN code, and a landmark where available.
  • Free delivery applies to orders of ₹499 or more. The cart and checkout show any delivery charge before you place an order.
  • The order confirmation shows an estimated delivery date, currently two days after purchase. This is an estimate and may change if delivery is delayed.

Returns & exchanges

  • Request a return or exchange within 7 calendar days of delivery.
  • Please keep the product unused and include its accessories and original packaging. Include your order number when contacting support.
  • Contact customer support to request help with a return or exchange. Requests are subject to review and product eligibility.

ABOUT INFINIX SHOPEE

Good things for everyday life.

Infinix Shopee is a multi-vendor shopping store serving customers across India.

01 · SHOP

For customers

Create an account or sign in with a username or email. Search and browse products, add items to your cart, choose a delivery location, and place orders. Returns & orders keeps purchase history and delivery status together.

02 · SELL

For sellers

Register as a seller, list products with a description and specifications, then manage or remove your own listings. Seller order tools show delivery details and let sellers update order status.

03 · ADMIN

For administrators

The admin interface provides marketplace oversight, including reviewing listings and managing products. Admin access is available through the seller sign-in flow.

04 · DETAILS

Shopping features

Prices and delivery charges are shown in Indian rupees (INR). Free delivery is offered on orders of ₹499 or more. Order confirmation shows an estimated delivery date two days after purchase, and returns and exchanges are available within 7 days.

05 · PERSONALIZE

Your experience

Switch between light and dark themes, set a delivery location, and use the responsive storefront on desktop or mobile.
